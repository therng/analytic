# Product Definition

## Project Name

Analytic

## Description

Trading analysis and monitoring with technic

## Problem Statement

Algo fund investors need a way to monitor and compare trading accounts across multiple MT5 bots — currently there's no easy way to see how your trading performance stacks up against others in real time.

## Target Users

Algo Fund Investors — people running or investing in algorithmic trading funds who need visibility across multiple accounts without switching between MT5 terminals.

## Key Goals

- เปรียบเทียบการเทรดของเรากับเพื่อน (Compare your trading performance against peers)
- Real-time monitoring of equity, balance, and open positions
- Historical analysis with visual charts and performance metrics
